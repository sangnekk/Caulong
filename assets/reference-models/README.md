## Nonprofit scope
Human clarified nonprofit project. CC BY-NC may be usable only when the actual use is noncommercial; storefront checkout/sales promotion from the earlier brief must be resolved. See https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/ . Nonprofit status does not waive price, download authentication, attribution, trademarks or resale restrictions. Before hosting paid GLB on a public web viewer, confirm the chosen license permits that distribution and required asset-extraction safeguards. Pre-rendered frames/video may be simpler for a NANFU-style scroll story, but are not a substitute for a valid asset license.
